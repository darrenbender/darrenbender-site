Indoor Air Quality

Breathe Cleaner, Healthier Air

Our technicians are experts in Indoor Air Quality, offering cutting-edge solutions developed with NASA technology.

Air Scrubber by Aerus®

Air Scrubber purification products use ActivePure® technology, originally developed in cooperation with NASA for the International Space Station. Proven to destroy up to 99% of all surface contaminants.

Working through your heating and air conditioning unit, the Air Scrubber significantly reduces dust, pet dander, odors, and airborne irritants throughout your home.

🔬

Reduce Allergens & Odors

Common smells such as pet odor, cigarette smoke, paint fumes, mold smells, and the occasional burnt food odor can all be significantly reduced. If you suffer from sneezing, itchy or watery eyes during allergy season, dust, mold, pollen, and many other pollutants can be addressed. ActivePure® Technology uses specialized light waves along with a proprietary catalytic process that creates scrubbing molecules of oxygen and hydrogen to purify your air.

Contaminants Follow You Home

Contaminants from school, daycare, or the office follow us home on hands, clothes, and shoes, landing on doorknobs, countertops, and floors. The Air Scrubber by Aerus® contains ActivePure® Technology, proven to destroy up to 99% of all surface contaminants — giving your family true peace of mind.

About Us

Meet the Family Behind Texan Mechanical

👪

The Wallman Family

Meet Michael and Kristin Wallman and family. They are high school sweethearts, raised right here in Fort Bend County and still proud Fort Bend residents. They have four beautiful children — Kamryn, Zackary, Maverick, and Maddyn — who all help out in some way, shape, or form with the family business.

Michael and Kristin both began their careers in the HVAC field while still in high school. They were blessed with the opportunity to work for one of the largest HVAC and Controls corporations in the world, specializing primarily in commercial HVAC service and maintenance.

In 2013, they started their own company, focusing their service towards residential and light commercial service, maintenance, and installation. Over a decade later, they are proud to continue serving their hometown and surrounding areas.

🎐

In Loving Memory of Maddyn

Our Maddy Girl was the caboose of our family and had big dreams. Only God knows why he needed her more than we did, but we know she is in Heaven watching over us and we will live for her and continue her legacy for the rest of our lives on this side.

We thank all of our customers, family, and friends in this amazing community for standing with us and supporting us through this extremely painful experience. We miss our beautiful girl beyond words every day, every hour. We know that we have the most precious guardian angel watching over and protecting us and our Texan Mechanical family. 🎐
